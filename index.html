What is FAMUN?

FAMUN (Famagusta Model United Nations) is a student led conference bringing Model United Nations back to Famagusta after many years. It brings young people together to discuss global issues, exchange ideas and experience diplomacy, debate and cooperation. For us, FAMUN is not just a conference, but a new beginning for youth engagement in our city.

Our Vision

Our vision is to make Famagusta a place where young minds meet, different perspectives are welcomed and meaningful ideas turn into action. We hope to inspire a generation that looks beyond borders and works towards a better future.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage young people to think critically, express themselves confidently and understand the world from different perspectives. Through debate, diplomacy and cooperation, we aim to create an environment where every participant has the opportunity to learn and grow.

Our Values

We value diplomacy, unity, inclusivity, responsibility and cooperation. We believe that every voice deserves to be heard, differences should bring us together rather than divide us, and meaningful progress begins with mutual understanding.

Why FAMUN?

FAMUN is an opportunity to develop new skills, meet people with different perspectives and become part of something meaningful. As MUN returns to Famagusta after many years, we hope to build a tradition that inspires future generations of young people to engage, question and make a difference.
